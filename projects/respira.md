**Role:** main developer, designer and documenter on a team of six.
**Stack:** Python, machine learning.

## The problem

A spirometry test is only as good as the effort that produced it. A blow that starts
late, ends early, or is interrupted by a cough gives a number that looks like lung
disease but is really just a bad manoeuvre. Judging that normally takes a trained
technician standing next to the patient.

RESPIRA puts both halves on a phone: assess whether the manoeuvre was acceptable,
then interpret the lung function it measured.

## What I did

- Built and trained the models for quality assessment and interpretation.
- Designed the app and the interface patients and staff see.
- Wrote the project documentation, and coordinated planning and delivery across
  a team of six.
